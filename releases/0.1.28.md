# Jackalope 0.1.28

Status: ready
Date: 2026-10-29

## Highlights

- Agent questions get room to answer. A waiting question takes most of the task
  page, and you can drag the handle beneath it to resize it. Number keys pick an
  option and Enter sends. If a task stops with a question still open, choose an
  answer and the task continues with it.
- The app and the `jackalope` terminal stay in step. Answering, pausing,
  finishing, queuing a message, editing a plan or deciding a deviation in one
  shows in the other right away. A message you have half typed follows the
  conversation between them.
- Finish work from the terminal. `/merge` and `/pr` deliver a conversation's work
  after a confirmation, `/unqueue` takes back a message that has not reached an
  agent, and `/status` sums up the project, the work and what needs you.

## Improvements

- Results that are plans offer **Review plan** and **Implement this plan**
  instead of a merge, and plan details at the top read as a short list.
- The sidebar says why work needs you, such as a waiting question or a failed
  check. Close finished work from it one row at a time or with **Close all**;
  it stays on the Work page and returns when it needs you again.
- Drag to resize the sidebar and the Review tab's file list. Sizes are
  remembered.
- The terminal answers questions that take several choices (Space ticks, or
  type `1,3`), shows tabs only for conversations that need you or that you
  opened there, announces other conversations that start needing you, and
  highlights what can be clicked under the pointer.
- The terminal greets you with a sweep of light across its wordmark, says how
  long finished work took, and stays quicker in long conversations while agents
  work. `@` mentions are blue, apart from plan mode's green.

## Fixes

- In the terminal, a typed reply to a permission request can no longer deny it
  by accident, and a question answered in the app no longer stays on screen
  where a choice could reach the next question.
- The task page no longer shows its own "Your answer is needed" notice while you
  are looking at that task.
- Work that changed no files no longer offers **Review and merge**.

## Known issues

- Desktop control on Wayland needs a desktop that offers the remote desktop
  portal; other Wayland desktops are not supported yet.
- Bot computers need Docker for a local sandbox. Jackalope-hosted computers are
  not available yet.
- Recursive Flint needs Ollama 0.40.8 or newer.
- Codex CLIs too old for your selected model fail with the provider's "requires a
  newer version of Codex" message. Update Codex, or choose another agent.
- Signed installation and updates still need validation on each platform.

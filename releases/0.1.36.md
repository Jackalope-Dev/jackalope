# Jackalope 0.1.36

Status: ready
Date: 2026-11-16

## Highlights

- **Back and Forward.** Two buttons beside the app name step through the pages,
  projects and settings categories you visited. Alt+Left and Alt+Right (Cmd+[ and
  Cmd+] on Mac) and a mouse's side buttons do the same.
- **Settings stay in Settings.** Agents & accounts, Routing, Tools, Connected apps
  and Devices now open inside Settings instead of taking you to another page.

## Improvements

- Routing has more room between the project switch and its options, and between
  the note about the app default and its button.

## Fixes

- A site icon fetched for a project shows in the icon dialog straight away,
  instead of only after closing and reopening it.

## Known issues

- Back and Forward return to the page and project, not to the task that was open
  in Work.
- Back and Forward and the pages inside Settings are new in this beta and have
  not yet been accepted in an installed app.
- Followed pull requests, the ACP agent list, bot sharing and reading replies
  aloud have not yet been accepted in an installed app.
- The phone companion is not in the App Store or Play Store yet. Installed phone
  builds pair only over HTTPS, such as a Tailscale address.
- Desktop control on Wayland needs a desktop that offers the remote desktop
  portal.
- Signed installation and updates still need validation on each platform.

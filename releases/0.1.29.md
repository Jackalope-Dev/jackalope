# Jackalope 0.1.29

Status: ready
Date: 2026-10-31

## Highlights

- A denser workspace built around your projects. The sidebar lists each project
  with the work that needs you first, then running and recent work with its
  agent and live status. Search moves into the title bar and the project and
  branch move to the status bar, so more of the window goes to the work itself.
- The window is frosted on Windows 11 and macOS, with every label kept readable
  over any wallpaper. Turn it off under Settings → Appearance → Frosted window.
- Agents show their work. Each expected outcome says how strong the agent's
  evidence is, and Jackalope checks cited files, test output and screenshots and
  lowers claims it can't back up. **Challenge this change** asks other agents,
  preferably from another model family, to break a result, and only failures
  they reproduced count.
- Compare attempts with a judge. A different agent can judge compared attempts
  against your request and recommend one, and **Keep and combine** drafts a
  follow-up that brings the best parts of the others into the one you keep.

## Improvements

- After you have been away, **Since you left** names what needs you and shows
  what finished or failed, screenshots first, with each decision's evidence and
  risk.
- Plan mode checks facts in your code instead of asking you about them, and
  every question it still asks says why only you can answer it.
- **Sharpen** rewrites a draft into a deeper version of the same request, in
  the app or with `/sharpen` in the terminal, and Undo brings your draft back.
- Tasks show how the agent understood your request, and long or unattended work
  keeps a **Decision log** of what the agent chose and why.
- **Make it a check** turns a lesson agents keep missing into a lint, type, test
  or CI check, and a feature map points review screenshots at the pages your
  change touched.
- The Triage bot template now triages reports and tries to reproduce them.
- Buttons, fields, tabs and lists are more compact with a mouse or trackpad and
  keep larger targets on touch screens.
- Choosing a project during setup shows a quick overview of its size, languages
  and latest commit, and you can go back to your workspace from adding a project.
- New installs follow your system's light or dark setting. Settings has a
  button in the title bar, and the theme picker there is off by default.
- Connected apps has a clearer three-step setup.

## Fixes

- Long work titles no longer widen the Work list.
- Onboarding's mascot speech bubble is no longer cut off by its card.

## Known issues

- Desktop control on Wayland needs a desktop that offers the remote desktop
  portal; other Wayland desktops are not supported yet.
- Bot computers need Docker for a local sandbox. Jackalope-hosted computers are
  not available yet.
- Recursive Flint needs Ollama 0.40.8 or newer.
- Codex CLIs too old for your selected model fail with the provider's "requires a
  newer version of Codex" message. Update Codex, or choose another agent.
- Frosted glass on macOS has not been checked on a device yet.
- Signed installation and updates still need validation on each platform.

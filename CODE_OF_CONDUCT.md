# Community guidelines

Jackalope welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
These guidelines apply to project issues, pull requests and other community
spaces managed by the maintainers.

- Be respectful, constructive and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for questions, disagreement and corrections.
- Do not harass, threaten or discriminate against others, or post sexual content.
- Do not publish another person's private information without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant; avoid spam and unsolicited promotion.

Report conduct concerns privately to [contact@jackalope.dev](mailto:contact@jackalope.dev).
Include relevant links and context without sharing unnecessary personal information.
Maintainers will limit disclosure to what is needed to review and address the report.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation
in response to these guidelines. They will consider the context and impact of the
conduct. Contact the same address if you believe a decision should be reconsidered.

Report software vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
